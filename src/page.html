available still waiting. The chart's time axis is logarithmic, from one minute to two weeks after the source tarball appeared on cache.ruby-lang.org.

Each source is checked at most once a minute. Clock times and dates on this page are in your browser's time zone. "Last checked" is the oldest check among a row's releases that are still waiting, or the check that found the last one.

Row times are T+, counted from the moment that release's source tarball appeared on cache.ruby-lang.org, the earliest anyone could have shipped it. A time marked ≤ is when this page first saw the release at a source that publishes no timestamp, so the real time may be earlier. Unmarked times come from the source's own timestamp, or from a check that had previously found the release missing.

Based on How fast can you actually patch Ruby? by Dennis Paagman, which measured the 4.0.9 rollout by hand.

Missing an installer, image or platform? The source is at github.com/speedshop/rubywatch. Open a pull request there to add another release channel.
